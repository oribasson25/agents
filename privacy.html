Privacy Policy

8Legs.ai, at www.8legs.world, is a platform for building AI chatbots. You describe what a chatbot should do, give it documents and tables to answer from, and publish it to a website widget, WhatsApp or email. This page says what the platform collects while you do that, why, where it is kept, and how to get rid of it.

Last updated 21 September 2026. 8Legs.ai is the product; www.8legs.world is where it runs. Operated by Ori Basson (“we”). Questions: oribasson25@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

Your account

A username, a password (stored only as a salted scrypt hash — we cannot read it), and a phone number, which is how an administrator reaches you about the account. Sign-up is by invitation, so we also keep a record of which invitation code opened your account.

What you build

The chatbots you configure, their prompts and skills, the documents and files you upload, the websites you ask a chatbot to crawl and the text pulled from them, and the tables of rows you keep. This is your content. We store it so the platform can show it back to you and so your chatbots can answer from it.

Conversations

Messages exchanged with your chatbots — by you while testing, and by the people who talk to a published chatbot on your website or over WhatsApp — are stored in full, with the tools each answer used. That is what the Interactions screen shows you. If the people who talk to your chatbot type personal information into it, that information is stored here too; deciding what a chatbot asks for is up to you, and so is telling those people about it.

Credentials you connect

Your Anthropic or OpenAI API key, and the access tokens for any Gmail mailbox, WhatsApp number or GitHub repository you connect. These are held so the platform can act as you asked it to, and are never shown to anybody but you.

Operational records

Sign-in times and server-side error logs, which include the request that failed. No advertising identifiers, no tracking pixels, no analytics on visitors.

2. Google user data, specifically

Connecting a mailbox is optional, and nothing else on the platform depends on it. When you do connect one, 8Legs.ai asks Google for exactly two things:

ScopeWhat it allowsWhat we do with it
gmail.send Sending mail as you. It grants no ability to read, search, delete or modify anything in the mailbox, and Gmail offers no narrower scope for sending. A chatbot of yours sends an email — a booking confirmation, a quote, a reply — at the moment it decides one is needed, or when you ask it to.
email Reading the address of the account you just connected. Showing you which mailbox a chatbot is connected to, so you can tell two apart.

We store the resulting access token, refresh token and the mailbox address, keyed to the one chatbot you connected it to. We do not read your mail. We do not have a scope that would let us. The body of an email your chatbot sends is kept in that conversation's record, the same as any other message it wrote.

Limited Use. 8Legs.ai's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Specifically: Google user data is used only to provide the sending feature you connected it for; it is never sold, never transferred to anyone except as needed to provide that feature or where required by law; it is never used for advertising; and no human reads it except with your explicit consent, to resolve a support issue you raised, or where required by law.

Disconnecting. The Disconnect button on the chatbot's Tools tab deletes the stored tokens and the mailbox address immediately. You can also revoke the permission from Google's side at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops it working whatever we hold.

3. Who else sees it

We do not sell anything and we run no advertising. Data reaches these services because the platform cannot work without them:

  • Anthropic and OpenAI — whichever you configured. The prompt for each turn goes to them: your chatbot's instructions, the relevant passages from your documents, and the conversation so far. They process it to generate the reply. Whose key it is, and therefore whose terms apply, is yours.
  • Neon — the Postgres database where everything above is stored.
  • Vercel — hosting for the application and its server functions.
  • Google — only the mail you send through a connected mailbox.
  • Meta / WhatsApp Business — only if you connect a WhatsApp number, and only the messages on it.
  • GitHub — only if you connect a repository.

Beyond those, we disclose data only when the law requires it.

4. Where it is kept, and for how long

Everything sits in one Postgres database, encrypted in transit. It is kept for as long as your account exists, because it is your account — a chatbot with its documents deleted is not a chatbot. Deleting something in the interface deletes the record: removing a document, a table row, a chatbot or a conversation removes it for good, and deleting a chatbot takes its documents, conversations and connected channels with it.

Server error logs are pruned on a rolling basis and are not part of your content.

5. Deleting your account

Ask an administrator, or write to oribasson25@gmail.com from the address associated with the account. Deleting a user deletes their chatbots, documents, tables, conversations, connected channels and stored credentials. It cannot be undone, and we keep no shadow copy.

6. Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy of it, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Most of it you can already read and change yourself on the screens where you put it there. Write to oribasson25@gmail.com for the rest; we answer within 30 days.

7. Children

8Legs.ai is a tool for businesses and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child's data has ended up here, write to us and we will remove it.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed, never stored. Access to the application requires a signed session token, and every request is checked against the account that owns the thing being touched. Traffic is HTTPS end to end. No system is perfect: if a breach affects your data, we will tell you.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change it materially, the date at the top changes and we say so in the application. The current version is always the one at this URL.
